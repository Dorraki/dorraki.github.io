01 — About

From research papers
to clinical products.

Mohsen Dorraki is an AI researcher, engineer and educator specialising in machine learning and clinical AI. He is a Lecturer at Adelaide University and previously worked as a Research Fellow and Lead Machine Learning Scientist across the Australian Institute for Machine Learning and SAHMRI.

His work translates AI into real-world healthcare: cardiovascular risk prediction, medical imaging, physiological signal analysis and clinical decision support. He has led and contributed to more than $1.7 million in research funding and has built AI technologies deployed with hospitals, research institutes and medical technology companies.

He is also the co-founder and CTO of Pi MedTech, where he leads development of AI-powered digital health products.

    Pi MedTechImaging → Graph AI

    PiNet

    See the structure hidden in an image.

    PiNet turns microscopy, CT and X-ray images into graph representations. Each junction becomes a node and each strand becomes an edge, so tissue and bone microstructure can be measured with network science. The method was validated in peer-reviewed research in Nature's Communications Biology.

    AIMLClinical Risk AI

    Circulatory Mortality Risk Profile

    SEAL-ML, a clinical AI for cardiovascular risk.

    Enter a patient's profile and the model estimates their circulatory mortality risk and shows which factors drive it, so clinicians can see why it gave that score. Developed in my lab at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning.
